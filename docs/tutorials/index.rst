.. _tutorials:

*********
Tutorials
*********

Tutorials are **learning-oriented**: lessons that take a newcomer by the hand
through a series of steps to complete a small project. They introduce CHIMERApy
by doing, and do not assume prior knowledge of the algorithm.

If you already know what you want to achieve, see the :ref:`how-to-guides`
instead.
